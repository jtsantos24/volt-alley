HOW TO RIDE

THROTTLE Hold the big pad to build speed. Let go and the bike coasts. It rides the hills and launches off steep crests.

TURBO A hotter shove. The core climbs. Fill the gauge and boost locks until it cools. Cyan pads reset heat.

LEAN In the air, hold BACK or FWD to flip. The ring is your nose. Square it up before you hit. A full turn that lands level still counts.

TRICKS A clean flip takes 0.5s off the clock per rotation, plus 0.5s for a multi-flip and 0.5s more for big air, and you get a short speed boost. A crash pays nothing.

GATES Jump the low barriers and the glowing beams. Rocks scrape your speed if you stay on the ground.

CRASH A bad landing or a gate you clip costs 3 seconds. You respawn past it, slower, and the clock keeps the penalty.

FINISH Cross the Sector 9 gate. Your best time stays on this phone.

Keys: D, Right, or Space throttle. Shift turbo. A, Left, or Up lean back. Q or Down lean forward. P pause.
