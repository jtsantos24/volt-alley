HOW TO RIDE

GAS The big right pedal builds speed. Let go and the bike coasts along the hills and launches off steep crests.

BRAKE The big left pedal scrubs speed on the ground. In the air it leans the nose back. The small FWD button leans the nose forward.

BOOST The round button by the gas pedal. The core climbs. Fill the heat dial and boost locks until it cools. Cyan pads reset heat.

TRICKS A clean flip takes 0.5s off the clock per rotation, plus 0.5s for a multi-flip and 0.5s more for big air, and you get a short speed boost. A crash pays nothing.

RIVALS NYX, VEX, and ION ride the same line. The badge is your place. The bar shows the pack. The number on the right is meters to the next marker.

COINS Grab the glowing chips on the line. The count stays on this phone.

GATES Jump the low barriers and the glowing beams. Rocks scrape your speed if you stay on the ground. A bad landing costs 3 seconds.

Keys: D, Right, or Space gas. S brake. Shift boost. A, Left, or Up lean back. Q or Down lean forward. P pause.
